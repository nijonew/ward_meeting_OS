# Ward Meeting OS
## Project Charter
### Version 1.0 (Draft)

---

# Project Vision

Ward Meeting OS is a configurable meeting management platform designed to help wards of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints prepare, conduct, publish, and archive meetings using a single source of truth.

The platform combines planning, collaboration, meeting execution, and publication into one cohesive system, reducing administrative burden while improving communication and preserving historical records.

Ward Meeting OS is intended to serve both current ward leaders and future leadership by providing continuity, organization, and efficient workflows.

---

# Mission Statement

Create a meeting operating system that enables ward leadership to spend less time managing meetings and more time ministering to people.

Technology should simplify administration—not become another administrative burden.

---

# Guiding Principles

## 1. Single Source of Truth

Information should only be entered once.

Every assignment, announcement, hymn, speaker, prayer, and meeting detail should automatically appear wherever it is needed.

Duplicate data is avoided whenever possible.

---

## 2. Simplicity for the User

The application should feel simple even though it manages complex relationships.

Users should never need to understand the underlying data model.

---

## 3. Build for Future Leadership

Leadership changes frequently.

The application should preserve institutional knowledge while remaining easy for new leaders to adopt.

No individual should become the "keeper of the spreadsheets."

---

## 4. Meetings First

The application exists to support meetings.

Every feature should improve one or more of the following:

- Meeting preparation
- Meeting execution
- Meeting communication
- Meeting follow-up
- Meeting history

---

## 5. Public by Design, Private by Necessity

Only appropriate information should be publicly available.

Private planning information must remain protected.

Public meeting information should require little or no technical knowledge to access.

---

## 6. Configuration over Customization

Meeting behavior should primarily be controlled through configuration rather than custom programming.

New wards should be able to adopt the platform without modifying source code.

---

## 7. Long-Term Sustainability

The project should remain understandable and maintainable years from now.

Architecture decisions should favor clarity over cleverness.

---

# Project Goals

## Reduce Administrative Burden

Replace multiple spreadsheets, documents, emails, and paper agendas with one integrated platform.

---

## Improve Meeting Quality

Provide conductors and participants with the information they need in the format they need.

---

## Improve Communication

Automatically publish appropriate meeting information to ward members.

---

## Preserve History

Maintain searchable records of meetings, assignments, announcements, and leadership decisions where appropriate.

---

## Enable Future Growth

Support additional meeting types, wards, and future functionality without redesigning the system.

# Problems We Intend to Solve

Current ward administration often relies on:

- Multiple spreadsheets
- Multiple Google Docs
- Emails
- Text messages
- Paper agendas
- Individual knowledge
- Repeated manual entry

Ward Meeting OS seeks to replace disconnected tools with one integrated workflow.
